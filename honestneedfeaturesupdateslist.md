Here’s a mission statement designed for the Battle Buddy / Veterans section of HonestNeed, written to speak both to veterans and to people who want to support them.
Writing
🇺🇸 OUR MISSION — NO VETERAN STANDS ALONE
Our mission is simple but urgent: to make sure no veteran has to face life’s hardest moments alone.
Every day, veterans across America struggle with loneliness, isolation, trauma, transition, financial hardship, relationship challenges, loss of purpose, and other difficulties that can become overwhelming. According to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, suicide remains a serious public-health issue within the veteran community. Behind every statistic is a real person—a veteran who served, a family that loves them, and a community that can make a difference.
That is why we are building a community where veterans can find real human connection and ongoing support—not only during a crisis, but before a crisis ever happens.
THE BATTLE BUDDY MISSION
In the military, you learned the importance of watching out for the person beside you. We believe that principle should continue long after someone takes off the uniform.
Our goal is to create a Battle Buddy support network that helps veterans connect with other veterans and supportive members of the community based on their experiences, interests, location, hobbies, activities, and the type of friendship or support they are looking for.
Sometimes someone needs a person to talk to.
Sometimes they need someone to grab a cup of coffee with.
Sometimes they need another veteran who understands where they have been.
Sometimes they need a group of people reminding them that they still belong.
And sometimes they need immediate professional crisis support.
We want veterans to have pathways to one-on-one peer connection, Battle Buddies, group support, community activities, mentorship, friendship, and professional crisis resources when needed.
SUPPORT SHOULDN’T BEGIN AT THE MOMENT OF CRISIS
Our vision is a support system available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, where technology helps connect people but human beings support human beings.
Veterans should be able to tell their story, describe their military background if they choose, share their interests and hobbies, and explain what kind of connection they are looking for.
Then we can help bring people together.
Veteran to veteran.
Battle Buddy to Battle Buddy.
Community to veteran.
One person to another.
We also invite civilians, businesses, nonprofits, churches, veteran organizations, volunteers, counselors, mentors, employers, donors, and community leaders to stand with our veterans.
You do not have to have served in the military to serve someone who did.
OUR PROMISE
We will work toward a community where asking for connection is a sign of strength, where veterans can build meaningful relationships before reaching a breaking point, and where someone who is struggling has somewhere to turn.
No veteran should have to fight their hardest battle alone.
You served for us. Now it is our turn to stand with you.
🇺🇸 FIND A BATTLE BUDDY • BECOME A SUPPORTER • JOIN A GROUP • HELP A VETERAN
If you are in immediate danger, call 911. Veterans and their loved ones experiencing a crisis can contact the Veterans Crisis Line by calling 988 and then pressing 1, texting 838255, or using the Veterans Crisis Line online chat.
